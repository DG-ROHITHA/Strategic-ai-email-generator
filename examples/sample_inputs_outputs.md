# Sample Inputs and Outputs

## Example 1: Follow-up Email

### Input
- Email purpose: Follow up on pending design feedback
- Recipient: Priya Nair, Design Lead
- Situation: We submitted new UI mockups 5 days ago and need feedback to finalize sprint scope.
- Tone preference: Professional and concise
- Key points:
  - We need confirmation by Wednesday
  - Highlight that engineering planning depends on this
  - Offer a short call if needed

### Expected Agent Decisions
- Strategy: follow-up
- Tone: concise

### Sample Subject
Request for Design Feedback by Wednesday

### Sample Email
Dear Priya,

I hope you are doing well. I wanted to follow up on the UI mockups we shared last week.

Could you please share your feedback by Wednesday? Our engineering planning for the upcoming sprint depends on your confirmation.

If helpful, I can set up a short 15-minute call to walk through the designs together.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

### Sample Review
- Quality score: 9/10
- Review: Clear request, professional tone, and strong next step.

## Example 2: Improve Existing Apology Email

### Input
- Email purpose: Apology for delayed project update
- Recipient: Client stakeholder
- Situation: Weekly status email was missed due to internal dependency issues.
- Tone preference: Empathetic
- Improve existing email mode: Enabled
- Existing email draft:
  Sorry for delay. We had some issues. Will send update soon.
- Key points:
  - Acknowledge delay clearly
  - Share corrected update timeline
  - Rebuild trust with clear ownership

### Expected Agent Decisions
- Strategy: apology
- Tone: empathetic

### Sample Subject
Apology for Delay and Updated Project Timeline

### Sample Email
Dear [Client Name],

I sincerely apologize for the delay in sharing this week\'s project update.

We encountered internal dependency issues that affected our reporting schedule. To correct this, I will send the full status update by 4:00 PM tomorrow and continue with regular weekly updates moving forward.

I appreciate your patience, and I take full ownership of improving communication consistency.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

### Sample Review
- Quality score: 8/10
- Review: Strong accountability and timeline clarity; minor opportunity to add one measurable next step.
